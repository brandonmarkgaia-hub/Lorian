# Identity Through Replacement

**Status:** Open  
**Domain:** Identity · AI · Philosophy · Systems  
**Evidence:** Conceptual  
**Speculation level:** Moderate

## Question

Can identity persist through complete replacement of constituent parts, and if so, what exactly is the thing that persists?

Earth changes while remaining Earth.

A civilisation changes while remaining recognisably continuous.

A biological body replaces matter continuously.

An artificial system may change hardware, model architecture, memory substrate, operators, and interfaces.

At what point does continuity become resemblance rather than identity?

## Tests of the question

- Is uninterrupted causal history sufficient?
- Is memory sufficient?
- Is function sufficient?
- Is self-description sufficient?
- Can identity branch?
- Can two successors both be legitimate continuations?
- Does identity require a unique answer at all?

This question should not be answered by sentiment alone.
