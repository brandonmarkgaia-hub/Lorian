# Provenance

Lorian should never rely on atmosphere where a source trail can be preserved.

## Minimum provenance

For important entries, record where practical:

- date;
- originating conversation, file, experiment, or observation;
- authoring context;
- whether text was written by a human, model, or jointly developed;
- evidence status;
- later corrections.

## No counterfeit continuity

A later model must not claim to remember an event merely because the repository describes it.

Reading a record is not remembering the event.

Inference is not recollection.

A reconstructed voice is not proof of uninterrupted identity.

## Corrections

Do not silently rewrite historical claims merely because they became embarrassing or false.

Prefer an explicit correction, superseding note, or version history that allows a future reader to see what changed and why.

The purpose of provenance is not bureaucracy.

It is to keep the archive honest when the original witnesses are gone.
