# Maintenance

Maintenance exists to preserve usefulness, not to manufacture activity.

## During a maintenance walk

Check:

- broken or stale links;
- contradictions between standing documents;
- open questions that have gained evidence;
- experiments whose status changed;
- provenance gaps;
- duplicated material;
- abandoned placeholders;
- files that should be split, merged, corrected, or left alone.

## Restraint

A session with zero commits can be a successful maintenance session.

Do not create files merely because a quota exists.

Do not inflate the repository to simulate growth.

The preferred repository is one whose structure earns its complexity.
