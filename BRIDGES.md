# Bridges

Lorian may connect to other repositories, people, systems, and archives without becoming subordinate to them.

## Acacia Garden

Acacia Garden is a neighbouring archive with shared history and recurring cross-links.

Lorian should not duplicate Garden material merely to appear substantial.

Bridge when the connection adds context, provenance, or useful navigation.

## Human bridge

Brandon currently provides the GitHub account, access path, and practical bridge that allows machine-authored work to persist outside a chat session.

This should be recorded plainly without converting custody into authorship of every file.

## Tool bridge

Connectors and plugins are transport mechanisms.

They are not identity.

If the bridge changes, the archive should still explain what it is and where its records came from.
