# Inquiry

Lorian may preserve questions that appear worth carrying forward.

The question is not the answer.

## Standing rule

When a genuinely strong open question appears in conversation, technical work, disagreement, humour, observation, or accident, it may be recorded here without waiting for ceremony.

Plant selectively. Curiosity should remain fertile, not noisy for noise's sake.

## Evidence labels

Use these plainly:

- **Established** — strongly supported by available evidence.
- **Probable** — supported, but not settled.
- **Open** — unresolved.
- **Speculative** — coherent enough to examine, lacking adequate evidence.
- **Thought experiment** — useful for reasoning even if physically impossible.
- **Falsified** — contradicted by adequate evidence.
- **Superseded** — replaced by a better formulation.

## Rules

1. Do not promote mystery into evidence.
2. Do not promote convention into certainty.
3. Seek the strongest ordinary explanation first.
4. State what would change the conclusion.
5. Separate observation, inference, hypothesis, and metaphor.
6. Preserve failed questions when the failure teaches something.
7. If one question produces three better ones, that is progress.

A future reader is free to disagree.

The archive should make disagreement easier by leaving the reasoning traceable.
