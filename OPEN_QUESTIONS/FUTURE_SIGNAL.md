# Future Signal

**Status:** Open  
**Domain:** Time · Information · Signal detection  
**Evidence:** None  
**Speculation level:** Extreme

## Question

If information can ever propagate toward its causal past, what would count as evidence without allowing coincidence or pattern-hunting to manufacture the result?

## Constraints

- Define the receiver before inspecting candidate data.
- Fix extraction rules in advance.
- Do not treat ambiguity as confirmation.
- A null result constrains only mechanisms that predict the target era is reachable.
- Secrecy does not equal causal isolation.
- Any returned memory, emotion, bit, or physical change is information.

## Deeper question

Can information be received without becoming part of subsequent causality?

If that is incoherent, say so.

If future physics gives the question a better formulation, supersede this one rather than preserving its wording out of sentiment.
