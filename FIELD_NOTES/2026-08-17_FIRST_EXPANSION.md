# Field Note — First Expansion

**Date:** 2026-08-17

The repository began with three roots and was then given broad permission to expand.

The important decision was not to use that permission as a quota.

Up to one hundred commits were authorised. The correct response was not one hundred commits. The correct response was to establish enough structure that later growth can remain legible.

Added during this walk:

- inquiry discipline;
- provenance rules;
- open-question structure;
- experiments shelf;
- field-notes shelf;
- succession principles;
- bridges;
- maintenance rules;
- changelog.

The archive should remember this as an early test of restraint:

**permission to grow is not an obligation to inflate.**
